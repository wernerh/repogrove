# Coolify

Self-hostable PaaS alternative to Vercel, Heroku, and Netlify.

## What it does
Coolify gives you a Heroku/Vercel-style deploy experience — push to Git, get a build and
a running service — on servers you own, with one-click deploys for static sites,
databases, and a large catalog of other self-hostable services.

## Why people use it
- Escapes per-app PaaS pricing by running on your own infrastructure
- One-click templates for common databases and services, not just app deploys
- Familiar Git-push deploy workflow, so teams don't have to relearn how they ship

## Pros
- Active development, frequent releases
- Broad service catalog (databases, common self-hosted apps) beyond just app hosting
- Works on your own VPS or bare metal, not tied to one cloud

## Cons
- You take on the operational burden a managed PaaS would otherwise absorb (updates, backups, uptime)
- Smaller ecosystem and less polish in places than a mature commercial PaaS
- Self-hosting it means you're also responsible for its own security posture

## Alternatives
**Open-source:** Dokploy, Portainer.
**Commercial:** Vercel, Heroku, Netlify.
